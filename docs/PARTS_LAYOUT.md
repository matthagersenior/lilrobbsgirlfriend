# Modular printed parts layout

- **PLA-001 lower front tub** — split chassis segment; motor mounting features based on selected gearbox.
- **PLA-002 lower rear tub** — bolted join with metal brackets/fasteners.
- **PLA-003 battery cradle** — low center, screw-down strap and insulated terminals.
- **PLA-004 electronics tray** — Pi standoffs, ESP32 and motor-driver separation, ventilation.
- **PLA-005 left body shell / PLA-006 right body shell** — rounded companion silhouette, removable.
- **PLA-007 head front / PLA-008 head rear** — camera aperture, eye light diffusers, microphone/speaker vents.
- **PLA-009 pan/tilt bracket** — servo geometry to be finalized after purchase.
- **PLA-010 carry handle** — metal-reinforced load path, not solely PLA snap-fit.
- **PLA-011 service covers and cable clips** — accessible battery/power maintenance.

This is a design specification, **not printable STL/3MF CAD yet**. Final dimensions depend on exact purchased parts.
