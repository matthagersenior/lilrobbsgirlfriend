# Revision F — easiest mounting + rounded companion shell

**Status:** CAD prototype published as downloadable ZIP/STLs in the ChatGPT conversation on 2026-10-17. Source, 21 unique printable STL designs, phone preview and printed-part quantities are included in that package. This file records the plan; GitHub does not yet host the ZIP binaries.

## Changes from Revision C
- Preserve the friendly style: rounded white/blue main body, split head shell, dark face, decorative eye rings and central camera aperture.
- Two split PLA base trays connect with two **M4 screwed bridge/splice strips** instead of glued chassis joints.
- Use **purchased metal L-brackets** matched to four geared motors; printed slotted plates are for low-cost fit-checks, not load-certified motor mounts.
- Use purchased **6mm-to-12mm hex adapters**, not printed couplers, for bought tires. [Pololu #2686](https://www.pololu.com/product/2686) is a verified example; 2 packs supply 4 hubs plus the M3 set screws/M4 wheel screws.
- [Pololu #1084 stamped 37D L-brackets](https://www.pololu.com/product/1084): 2 pairs for 4 motors. Includes the motor-side M3 screws. Brackets are for the Pololu 37D motor family; do **not** assume they fit the lower-cost Aslong alternative. Pololu warns the stamped brackets can bend under heavy load.
- Three printed spacer bands (4 units of the **chosen** band): **9mm** riser for 69mm apartment tires; **21mm** for 96mm everyday tires; **28mm** for 110mm rougher-terrain tires. This retains about 7–9mm nominal underside clearance on ideal hard flat ground. It needs physical verification before use.
- The outside body remains identical across wheel sets, but the brackets must also be repositioned/spacers changed. Swapping wheels alone will *not* maintain correct clearance.
- Removable electronics deck + battery cradle + split rounded face/head + friction-test cap. The Pi 5 **2GB** stays selected.

## Standardized assembly philosophy
1. Fit-test **one** metal bracket on **one** slotted motor adapter and its appropriate spacer. Adjust CAD once precise bought-part dimensions are known.
2. Print two base trays and two M4 splice bars; use through-bolts with washers and locknuts; metal backing reinforcement for loaded use.
3. Mount four bought metal motor brackets with common M3 fasteners and the selected height risers; install 12mm hex hubs and wheels last.
4. Secure purchased protected battery low in a restrained cradle; assemble driver, isolated Pi power and ESP32 safety/watchdog on a serviceable electronics deck.
5. Screw/snap-test cosmetic shell and head pieces; central camera opening remains a fit-check until Camera 3 and its tilting mechanism are measured.
6. Use a separately purchased **metal-anchored carrying webbing strap**. The printed trim labeled "strap_guide" is **NOT a load-bearing handle**.
7. Verify motor current limiting, physical latching E-stop, cut-off on comms loss and contact/cliff hazard detection before any autonomous floor operation.

## Prototype validation
- 21 distinct individual STL meshes generated by OpenSCAD, inspected with a triangle-mesh checker: each watertight; dimensions <= 256mm on each axis. Note individual parts still require correct print orientation and slicer review.
- **CAD-only:** incomplete powered electrical diagram, motor-to-plate fit, camera servo fit, hatch fasteners, load-rated strap attachments and real obstacle sensing. The exploded visualization is a mechanical concept, not a proof of final assembly.
