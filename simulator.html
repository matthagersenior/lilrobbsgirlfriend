Take the little rover for a spin.

Drive a virtual four-wheel, two-motor robot through a simple apartment-style course. Use the on-screen arrows or your keyboard. Tap a destination in waypoint mode to watch a basic simulated steering controller try to reach it. This is an illustrative sandbox, not a tested navigation or robotics physics engine.

Ready · Manual drive

No user accounts, camera feed, internet connection or real motor signals are involved.

Not a real robot controller. Motion, sensing, collisions, battery readings and emergency stop are virtual approximations. Physical Rev G hardware hasn't been assembled or validated, and the live robot has no network connection to this page.

Try it

  • Hold ↑ to drive ahead; hold ← or → to rotate in place, including zero-turn-style maneuvers.
  • Use the speed slider to limit movement, then practice avoiding furniture and the red hazard area.
  • Choose Waypoint and tap an open area. The simplified controller steers toward the target but does not plan around obstacles.
  • Press Virtual E-stop to latch the simulated drive off; press Reset to clear the latch and restart.
