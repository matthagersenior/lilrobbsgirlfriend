The idea

A companion that meets people where they are.

Designed with one-handed use and accessible interaction in mind, this project explores how a small mobile robot might offer connection, awareness and practical support without a complicated interface.

💬

Talk naturally

Planned GPT-powered conversation through a secure online service, with voice input and spoken replies. Not currently integrated.

🛞

Move with purpose

A four-wheel, two-motor concept for low-speed indoor mobility, with future follow and manual navigation modes.

📱

Simple controls

A proposed phone interface for steering, camera view and sentry functions, with prominent stop controls and one-handed operation.

📷

See the surroundings

Camera and sensor integration are planned for observation and assistance. Recognition alone must never determine safe motion.

🛑

Safety first

Independent motor watchdog, physical emergency stop, obstacle and edge sensing are engineering requirements before driving.

🧩

Built to evolve

Modular 3D-printed shells, accessible battery and electronics, and interchangeable wheel/motor components are design goals.

Questions

Good to know

Can I buy the robot now?

Not yet. This is an open design and development project, not a product listing or store. The bill of materials and real-world build have not been finalized.

Can I remotely drive it from this site?

No. No. There is no connected robot or live control service behind this page. You can drive the virtual rover with touch or keyboard controls, but it does not connect to hardware.

Does it already talk with ChatGPT?

Not yet. The plan calls for a secure backend integration with an OpenAI GPT model for normal conversation, but no working connection has been demonstrated. A consumer ChatGPT subscription is not an embedded robot API.

What does the estimated build cost cover?

An older Revision G worksheet estimated approximately $456.70 for selected priced components only, not a complete build. Several safety, mechanical and assembly items, tax, shipping and recurring API costs are not included. Do not treat that estimate as a purchase quote.

Where can I follow the design?

Review the public GitHub repository, latest CAD and build-decision documents. They show what is actually documented and where the engineering risks remain.
